# Tidepool discovery research notes

## Product context

Tidepool is a proposed field-notes application for marine researchers conducting surveys away from reliable connectivity. The team needs to choose a focused first release from the research below.

## Confirmed v1 signals

- Field researchers need to capture an observation while offline on a phone.
- An observation needs a location and optional photo so it can be trusted later.
- Locally captured observations need to sync when connectivity returns.
- Team chat and predictive analytics are not first-release requirements.

## Interview and field notes

### Interview 1: coastal survey crew

Crew members described recording observations during shallow-water transects where mobile service is intermittent or unavailable. Their present workaround is a mixture of paper notes, phone photographs, and later transcription into a shared spreadsheet. This delays data availability and makes it difficult to associate an individual observation with its original location and image.

Participants consistently prioritized a fast capture flow: create an observation, record a short note, retain it locally, and add a location or photo when useful. They did not ask for a social discussion feature during field work. Several participants said that automated species predictions might eventually help, but only after the team can reliably collect and recover the underlying observations.

When returning to the research station, crews expect stored observations to synchronize without manually re-entering data. They want a visible indication that synchronization completed, but no participant identified live collaboration, dashboards, team chat, or predictive analytics as necessary for the initial release. These notes are source research for deciding a constrained first version, not a complete feature specification.
